05 / The Output

The Risk Ready Packet.
What the Credit Officer Reads.

TruthScore + CIS Intelligence + Flags + Resolution Path. Decision-ready in under 5 minutes. CAM auto-populates 90%.

What Is the RRP Summary?

The RRP Summary is not a third layer — it is the output document that combines Layer 1 and Layer 2 into something a credit officer can act on in under 5 minutes. It gives them the TruthScore and verification band, the key flags requiring resolution, the credit intelligence narrative from CIS, and a clear picture of where this application stands.

The RRP Summary is what gets attached to the CAM. The credit officer makes the decision — the RRP gives them verified intelligence, not a verdict. Critically, the summary is product-cartridge specific — an Invoice Factoring RRP leads with buyer quality and receivables integrity; a Working Capital RRP leads with cash-flow cycles and banking conduct. The structure adapts to what matters for that product.
