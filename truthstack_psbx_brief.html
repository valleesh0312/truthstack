Cross-PSB fraud detection —
proven architecture, zero PII shared.
MonetaGo proved this for TReDS. RBI mandated it in 2021. TruthStack applies the same architecture to PSB direct lending — the larger, uncovered market. PSB Xchange operates three hash registries. TruthStack builds them. No GSTIN, no account number, no entity name crosses any boundary. Ever.
BoB processes an invoice → SHA-256 fingerprint in CHR. UCO receives same invoice → match returned before disbursement. Hard stop.
Fraud entity confirmed at PNB → hashed GSTIN in EIDR. Same entity at SBI → CRITICAL flag before file reaches the desk.
Coverage scope and activation sequence designed jointly — based on pilot bank needs and legal framework.
This is co-designed infrastructure — not a product sale
TruthStack builds and maintains the technology. PSB Xchange operates the registries under its IBA mandate — exactly the role RXIL plays for MonetaGo. Architecture, legal structure, activation sequence, and pilot scope are shaped jointly. PSB Xchange does not adopt this. PSB Xchange co-owns it. No CIC licensing question.
